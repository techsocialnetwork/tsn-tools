💸 Funding rates

Hourly funding from the info API. A positive rate means longs pay shorts. Annualized % multiplies that hourly rate by 24 × 365 and is illustrative, not a quoted yield. Impact spread is the gap between the API's bid and ask impact prices, not the top of the book. Mark vs oracle is (mark − oracle) / oracle.
