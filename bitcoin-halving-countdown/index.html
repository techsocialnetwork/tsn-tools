What is the Bitcoin Halving?

The Bitcoin halving is a programmed event that occurs every 210,000 blocks (approximately every 4 years). It cuts the block reward miners receive in half, reducing the rate at which new Bitcoin enters circulation. This creates artificial scarcity and has historically preceded major bull runs. The 2028 halving will reduce the block reward from 3.125 BTC to 1.5625 BTC.
