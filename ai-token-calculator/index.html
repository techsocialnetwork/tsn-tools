About This AI Token Calculator

Our AI token calculator helps developers and businesses estimate the cost of using large language models (LLMs). Simply paste your text, and we calculate the approximate token count and show pricing across 8 major AI models.

What Are Tokens?

Tokens are the basic units of text that AI models process. A token can be a word, part of a word, or even a single character. For English text, 1 token is roughly 4 characters or 0.75 words. Code and non-English languages may use more tokens per character.

Why Token Count Matters

  • API Costs: Most LLMs charge per million tokens consumed
  • Context Limits: Models have maximum token windows (4K to 1M tokens)
  • Performance: Longer prompts = slower responses and higher costs
  • Budgeting: Estimate monthly spend before scaling your AI application
