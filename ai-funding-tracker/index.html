Method, limits and what is not here

Loading…

  • Confirmed vs reported. “Confirmed (company)” means the company’s own announcement states the figure. “Reported” means a news outlet or unnamed sources did; those can change.
  • Amounts in other currencies (for example yuan) are shown as stated and are not converted, so they are left out of the USD size filter.
  • “Up to” and “more than” amounts are shown with those words; they are not exact totals.
  • Not included: talks that have not closed, loans and contracts, and non-AI rounds TSN also covered (fusion, nuclear, quantum, crypto-only).
  • Found an error? Tell TSN via tsnmedia.org and include the row and the source.
